I discovered dsda-doom in 2021 and immediately made some pull requests to it, since some behaviours on macOS weren't correct. This led me to developing dsda-launcher. I kept occasionally contributing to dsda-doom, and when kralab decided to retire, he asked me, fabiangreffrath and rfomin to be the new maintainers of the port.

Since fabian and rfomin have their hands full with Woof!, most of the responsibilities fell on me. I made a new [DoomWorld thread](https://www.doomworld.com/forum/topic/150766-dsda-doom-v0294-2025-09-026/), reworked the menu, implemented a clickable demo progress bar, and much much more.
